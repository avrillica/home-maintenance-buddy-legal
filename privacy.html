Privacy Policy — Home Maintenance Buddy

Last updated: August 2026

Home Maintenance Buddy is designed to work fully offline. The information below only applies to the optional features you choose to turn on (signing in, cloud backup, Household Sharing).

What we collect

  • Account info: if you create an account, your email address and password (or Google account identifier) are handled by our backend provider, Supabase, to sign you in.
  • Profile info (optional): phone number and home address, only if you choose to enter them. Phone number is used only to label local notification style — it is not sent anywhere or used for real SMS.
  • Home data: rooms, appliances, service history, and achievements you enter. This stays on your device unless you turn on cloud backup, in which case it's stored in your Supabase account so it can sync across your devices.
  • Photos and documents: manuals and receipts you attach stay on your device only. They are never uploaded unless you explicitly tap "Back Up Documents to Cloud."
  • Purchase records: if you buy a one-time upgrade, Google Play handles the payment. We store a record of the purchase (not your payment details) to unlock the features you paid for.
  • Household data: if you join or create a household, the members of that household can see the shared home data (rooms, appliances, service history) for that household.
  • Advertising: free-tier screens show banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device/advertising identifiers to serve ads; paid-tier accounts do not see ads.

How it's stored

Cloud data is stored with Supabase, a backend provider, with access restricted so only you (or, for household data, your household's members) can read it. Local data stays in this app's private storage on your device.

Your rights

You can delete your local data at any time from Settings. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data (backups, documents, profile info) at any time from Account & Sync → Delete Account. This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the Contact/Support screen in the app, or email us at support@homemaintenancebuddy.app.

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
