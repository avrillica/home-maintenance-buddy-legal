🏠 Home Maintenance Buddy

Track appliances, maintenance, and home repairs — organized and offline-first.

Home Maintenance Buddy is an Android app that helps homeowners stay on top of everything that keeps a house running: room and appliance tracking, maintenance reminders, service history, seasonal checklists, and guided home walkthroughs — all working fully offline, with optional cloud sync and household sharing.

Features

  • Room and appliance tracking with photos, warranty info, and service history
  • Maintenance reminders before things are actually due
  • Home Health score and an at-a-glance Overdue / Due Soon / Completed breakdown
  • Guided room-by-room walkthroughs and a seasonal task checklist
  • Optional cloud backup and Household Sharing
